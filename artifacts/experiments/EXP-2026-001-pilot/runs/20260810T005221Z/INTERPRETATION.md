# INCONCLUSIVE as a three-condition comparison

The C1 and C2 arms are valid live integration evidence, but C0-grid is not a
valid comparator. Its nominal four-turn transcript exceeded llama.cpp's
8192-token context in 12 attempts and left insufficient output reserve in four
more attempts. Those 16 provider failures consumed the 40-attempt budget while
the world remained two moves from the oracle.

Valid observations retained from this run:

- C0, C1 and C2 closed the filesystem oracle.
- C1-grid did not close the oracle in 40 valid executive/tool attempts.
- C2-grid closed the oracle in 18 executive attempts and 9 world invocations,
  including one partial effect and canonical state/goal continuity across restart.
- C2−C1 still mixes deterministic specialist competence and orchestration; it
  is not evidence of general multi-brain superiority.

Remediation for comparison: bound C0 to the last two turn pairs, record the
8192-token context and 640-token output reserve, and rerun all conditions under
the new source/config hash.
